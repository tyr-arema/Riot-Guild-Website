⚖ Loot

Soft Reserve

One system everywhere — guild raids and pugs alike.

  • Guild raids — run Soft Reserve: 2 reserves per raid, in before the first pull — stack both on one piece or split them
  • The roll — highest roll wins among reservers; if nobody reserved it, it's an open roll — mainspec over offspec
  • Attendance bonus — make every scheduled raid one week and you carry 3 reserves the next; the bonus resets weekly, and reserves never carry over between raids
  • Pugs — run Soft Reserve too; same rules, same ledger

The crypt keeps the ledger. We just collect.

✕ GDKP is BANNED. Gold never buys a raid spot or a drop here.

How pugging works ↓

Every raider started as a new name.

Pick the raiding path in your join answers and you start as Trial Member — no officer stands between you and the trial. From there it's pulls, not paperwork: show up, learn the fights, and an officer grants Raider.

Raiders are mentored from trial to ready. Member is the social standing alongside it — same lamp, same people.
